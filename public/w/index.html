Frequently asked questions

Before you enroll.

Do I need music industry experience?

No. The course starts with the fundamentals and is designed for independent artists, producers and managers at an early stage.

Does the course guarantee streaming income?

No. The program is educational and does not promise a particular financial or career result. Outcomes vary by catalog, audience, market and the work each participant applies.
